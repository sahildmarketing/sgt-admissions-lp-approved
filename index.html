About

About SGT University, Gurugram

SGT University, accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC, is committed to academic excellence, research and innovation. With 19 schools offering 200+ multidisciplinary programmes, it fosters entrepreneurship, global collaborations and groundbreaking research.

Education goes beyond the classroom. The university's healthcare ecosystem includes the multispeciality 1,000+ bed SGT Hospital and a superspeciality Ayurveda hospital with Panchakarma and Ksharasutra centres, a wellness centre and a herbal garden. Many faculty members are recognised in Stanford University's “Top 2% Scientists” list.

FAQs

SGT University Admission FAQs

Is SGT University NAAC accredited?

Yes. SGT University is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council).

What are SGT University's rankings?

SGT University is ranked 45 nationally, 19 in its zone and 4 in the state by IIRF, 48 in India by Outlook, and 100 in Pharmacy by NIRF. It is ranked 98 nationally in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings and 397 in South Asia in the QS Asian University Rankings, and holds a Diamond+ band in the MHW Ranking for mental health and well-being.

Where is SGT University located?

The campus is on Gurgaon-Badli Road, Chandu, Budhera, Gurugram, Haryana 122505, in Delhi NCR.

What courses does SGT University offer?

SGT University offers 200+ programmes across 19 schools, including MBBS, BDS, BAMS, B.Sc Nursing, BPT, B.Pharm, B.Tech, BCA, BBA, MBA, BA LLB, BJMC, B.Sc Agriculture and B.Ed, along with postgraduate and Ph.D programmes.

How do I get admission to MBBS or BDS at SGT University?

Admission to MBBS and BDS is based on NEET UG and the applicable counselling process. You need to have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology. A counsellor can guide you through the steps.

What is the admission process for other courses?

Submit a query, get counselling, fill in the application form online or offline, attend a personal interview, reserve your seat, and complete your documents. Some programmes also consider entrance scores such as JEE Main for B.Tech or CAT/MAT for MBA, or the SGT entrance test.

Does SGT University offer scholarships?

Yes. Scholarships are offered under the university's scholarship policy for each academic year. Your counsellor can tell you which ones you may qualify for.

What are placements like at SGT University?

Students get 100% placement support, with 275+ recruiters and a highest package of ₹42 LPA. Recruiters include Amazon, Google, Deloitte, Infosys, TCS, EY, Fortis and Medanta.
